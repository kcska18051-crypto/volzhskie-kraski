## Использованный промпт

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a premium industrial editorial photograph-like concept image for a Russian paint manufacturer's landing page. This is a clearly labelled demonstration visual, NOT documentary imagery of a real factory. Wide 3:2 landscape, 1536x1024 or larger. Extreme close-up inside a brushed cold stainless steel cylindrical mixing vessel: thick deep signal-red glossy paint folding in a beautiful powerful spiral around a steel mixing shaft, believable tactile viscous material and detailed steel rim. Vessel dominates right two thirds; left third is very dark deep navy #101D34 negative space, soft industrial shadows. Camera above at a low oblique angle, vessel cropped on right and lower edges for monumental scale. Restrained cinematic cool light on metal and rich red paint highlights, premium industrial art direction, photographic texture, no neon, no particles, no people, no logos, no text, no watermark. Composition must be striking even as a static image.
